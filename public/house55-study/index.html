How the outline closes

One consistent set of dimensions.

The prescribed modules produce the 21.30 m central span, 9.30 m and 12.40 m wings, 1.90 m left steps and 5.00 m right steps. All twenty clear interiors remain 2.70 × 6.00 m.

Each room has its own 20 cm side allowance, so adjacent clear interiors have 40 cm between them. The 7.40 m depth budgets 6.00 m for the interior and 1.40 m for its hall; additional front and rear wall thickness is not allocated.

The stair flight, landings, door and window symbols show a possible arrangement within the prescribed dimensions. Their detailed construction is still to be resolved.
