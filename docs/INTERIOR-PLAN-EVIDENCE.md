## Minimum useful input for one real floor

Start with **2. OG in Red House 55**, the selected pilot. A resident-drawn sketch can be useful if its measurements and unknown parts are explicit; a permission-cleared operator plan is stronger. The same checklist applies when other houses follow.

1. **Identity:** street number, floor label/index and whether the drawing represents the present layout. For an older house, include the boundary between occupied space and the unoccupied roof area.
2. **Geometry:** the complete floor perimeter, room and corridor outlines, stairs/lift/shared areas, and the location and width of each known door. Keep unknown regions visibly unknown.
3. **Registration:** a north arrow or two recognizable exterior reference points, plus at least one reliable dimension or a preserved scale bar. A perspective photograph needs rectification before measurement; two independent dimensions help check distortion.
4. **Height:** the floor's elevation relative to a known building reference, or a measured floor-to-floor relationship. Clear room ceiling height is a different measurement. Until height is verified, retain a draft rather than call its vertical placement measured.
5. **Labels and provenance:** actual room numbers only where confirmed; source/date and permission to publish the derived physical layout. Door hinge and swing are optional when unknown.

There is no need for resident names, current occupancy, contact details, access codes or private group links. A missing room number can remain “Unlabelled room”; an internal stable geometry ID is not a newly invented physical room number.

## Fit with the current data model

The public `interiors.json` still contains no approved floor records at the time of this research. [INTERIOR-FORMAT.md](INTERIOR-FORMAT.md) is the authoritative implementation contract. It supports per-floor elevations and simple polygons for rooms, corridors, stairs, lifts, shared and service spaces. The current door extension records a stable physical ID, two segment endpoints and a referenced space; an optional second space expresses reviewed adjacency. Hinge/swing can be omitted together.

Choose and preserve the polygon convention when tracing. **Clear-room boundaries separated by wall thickness do not share an edge.** Keep those measured polygons intact and omit `toSpaceId` until a wall/threshold representation supports their connection. Shared partition-line topology can support current two-space adjacency, but its polygon area must not be reported as measured clear floor area. Do not snap one convention into the other silently.

For future door popups, the public geometry should identify only the physical space/door. The private backend can resolve an explicitly opted-in resident association after authorization. A blank resident result must not announce that a room is vacant. Detailed wireframes and door selection can be implemented before any personal profiles exist.

## Reuse boundary

No open redistribution licence was established for the referenced architectural drawings, photographs or resident sketches. This study links them and records limited factual observations; it does not bundle their images or trace a proposed plan into the live dorm model. Official exterior geometry keeps its separate attribution. New approved interior data needs its own source and reuse record.
